# Nexus — Sample Questions (RAG-backed)

20 questions spanning all 40 ingested sources. Ask Nexus these directly (chat or `/rag/search`).

## HR & Policy
1. How many casual leave days am I entitled to per year, and do they carry over?
2. What's the process and documentation needed for sick leave over 3 days?
3. How many days of WFH can I take per week, and who approves it?
4. What's the maternity and paternity leave entitlement?
5. How much is the employee referral bonus, and when is it paid out?
6. When are performance reviews conducted, and what are the rating categories?

## IT Support
7. My VPN won't connect — what troubleshooting steps should I try before raising a ticket?
8. What's the SLA for a new laptop request, and how far in advance should I raise it for a new joiner?
9. Who do I contact for an email account lockout, and how long does it take to resolve?
10. What's the process to request access to a new SaaS tool like Figma?

## Finance
11. What's the reimbursement cap for cab travel per day, and how many days do I have to submit a claim?
12. Above what claim amount does an expense need CEO sign-off?
13. What are the standard payment terms for vendor invoices, and what documents does vendor onboarding require?
14. What does the Q1 vs Q2 2025 financial/BRS reconciliation show (per the BRS and Financials docs)?

## Marketing
15. What are the brand's primary and secondary colors, and who must review external decks before publishing?
16. At what campaign budget does COO sign-off become required?

## Lending / Credit Operations (new docs)
17. What are the key steps in the DIRECT process manual for loan sourcing?
18. What is CIBIL and how does it affect a customer's credit journey?
19. What's the NACH activation and curing process for a bounced/failed mandate?
20. Per the RBI Master Direction, what are the key compliance requirements for an NBFC (non-deposit taking, systemically important)?

---
**Note:** 15 policy PDFs (FL-*-POL-*, Attendance, Child Labour, Code of Conduct, Employee Grievance, Workers Organization, Travel policy) were scanned/image-only and skipped — no extractable text yet. Questions on those topics won't return results until OCR is added.
